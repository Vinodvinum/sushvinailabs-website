Our Story

Founded in 2025, Sushvin AI Labs was born from a simple observation: Mysuru's vibrant business community needed modern, accessible automation solutions tailored to local needs.

We saw clinics drowning in appointment calls, salons managing bookings on paper, and coaching centers struggling with manual follow-ups. Traditional automation was either too expensive, too complex, or built for enterprises—not for local businesses.

So we built something different: AI-powered automation that's affordable, easy to use, and delivers results in days, not months.

🚀

Our Mission

To make AI-powered automation accessible to every small and medium business in Mysuru, helping them save time, reduce costs, and focus on what they do best—serving their customers.

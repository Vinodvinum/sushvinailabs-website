Our Story

Sushvin AI Labs was founded on a simple observation: Mysuru's small and medium businesses are constantly struggling with repetitive tasks—managing customer inquiries, booking appointments, following up on leads, and handling administrative work.

We realized that automation technology existed but was either too expensive, too complex, or designed for large enterprises. So we decided to build something different: affordable, easy-to-implement AI solutions specifically designed for local Mysuru businesses.

Today, we help clinics, salons, coaching centers, and service businesses save 10+ hours per week while boosting customer satisfaction and revenue.
